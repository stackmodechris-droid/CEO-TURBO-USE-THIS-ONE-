EVENT LAST MINUTE — voice-over script (18.3 s, 9:16)
Ad: ads/hyperframes/build.mjs → id "event-last-minute" → ceoturbo.com/custom-company-t-shirts

Timeline is real, not "overnight": company shirts print in 2–4 business days and arrive
3–6 business days after they ship (src/config/site.ts). Don't promise faster in the voice.

VOICE
- Woman, 25–35, American, warm. A TikTok creator telling a friend what happened, not an announcer.
- Talk, don't read: run-on phrasing, a breath before the punchline, slight laugh on "oh my god".
- Imperfect is good: let "like" and "seriously" land loose. No studio-perfect diction.
- Arc: panic → embarrassed → surprised → relieved/proud → genuine recommendation.
- Pace: fast at the top, slows in scene 4, steady and warm at the end.

[SCENE 1 — 0.0–4.1s — on screen: "EVENT IS IN 2 WEEKS" / sticker "and i have ZERO shirts"]
(Panic, fast, a little breathless. Hit "two weeks" and "zero".)
"Okay so my event is in two weeks... and I have, like, zero shirts."

[SCENE 2 — 4.1–7.7s — on screen: "SPONSOR ASKED: 'WHERE'S THE MERCH?'"]
(Storytelling, embarrassed. Quote the sponsor in a slightly prim voice, then drop to a whisper-laugh.)
"Then the sponsor goes, 'where's the merch?' And I'm like... oh my god."

[SCENE 3 — 7.7–10.5s — on screen: "UPLOADED LOGO. DONE IN 2 MIN." / sticker "no minimum"]
(Surprised, picking up speed, a bit giddy. Lean on "two minutes".)
"So I uploaded our logo. Two minutes. Done."

[SCENE 4 — 10.5–14.5s — on screen: "SHOWED UP WITH DAYS TO SPARE." / sticker "prints in 2–4 days"]
(Slower, relieved, proud. Smile you can hear.)
"Printed in a few days, showed up with time to spare... and they look so good."

[SCENE 5 — 14.5–18.3s — end card: "NEVER PANIC AGAIN" / "Design yours"]
(Warm, sincere, not salesy. "Seriously" is a friend talking, not a pitch.)
"Seriously. Save yourself the panic. Design yours."
