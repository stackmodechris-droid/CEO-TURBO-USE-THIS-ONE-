# /get-more-reviews tutorial ad — 9:16 script

**Format:** your face in the top half, **ceoturbo.com/get-more-reviews** on a phone in the bottom half. You scroll and talk.
**Goal:** sell review cards. Every beat pushes toward the orange "Get my review cards" bar, which stays on screen the whole time.
**Emotion:** laugh (the Netflix joke) → "oh no, that's me" (who would you call?) → "I didn't know that" (the robots) → laugh again (the meme) → "I want that" (their name on the demo) → buy.

The poll listings are clearly an illustration. Don't call them real businesses, and don't promise a number of reviews. The calculator uses the viewer's own guess.

## Three opening hooks to A/B test (first 3 seconds)

1. **Netflix (humor):** "Your customers say they'll leave a review. Then they go home and watch Netflix."
2. **Poll (curiosity):** "Your sink just exploded. 212 reviews or 9 — who are you calling?"
3. **Robots (fear/curiosity):** "ChatGPT is recommending your competitor right now. Here's why."

Run each one as its own ad (`utm_content=hook_netflix|hook_poll|hook_robots`). Judge them by **Purchases** (Meta "Purchase" from the Shopify pixel), not clicks.

## Rules (keep the ad approved)

- Don't say "Google's card" or use the Google logo as your brand. The card links to *your* Google review page; we're not affiliated with Google.
- Never suggest discounts or freebies for reviews, or only asking happy customers. Google removes those reviews.
