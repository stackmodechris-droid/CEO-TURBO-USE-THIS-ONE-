# ChatGPT Prompts: 3 High-Converting Meta Ads (1080x1350)

## IMPORTANT IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS

All ads must be **1080x1350 (4:5 format)** for Facebook/Instagram Feed.

**CRITICAL SAFE ZONE RULES**:
- Keep ALL important text, logos, prices, products, CTAs, and website in the **middle 80%** of the canvas
- Leave **10% padding** (108px) on all four sides
- No text closer than **120px** from any edge
- No bleed stretch to edges
- Output: JPG max quality

---
