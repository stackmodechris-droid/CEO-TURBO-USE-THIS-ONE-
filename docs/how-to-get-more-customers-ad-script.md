# /how-to-get-more-customers: 9:16 tutorial ad script

**Format:** your face on the top half, ceoturbo.com/how-to-get-more-customers on a phone on the bottom half. You scroll while you talk.
**Story:** they get their own score first (a free win), then you walk through every step and show the nerd stuff behind it. It ends with "or I do all of it for you," and they book the call.
**Rule:** every number on screen is real. Don't round anything up when you say it out loud.

---

## Recording setup
- Record the phone screen, or Chrome DevTools at **390 × 844**, on the live page.
- **Before you record,** run the free check once with a real business name, like a friend's shop. The page remembers it, so the rank climb in step 2 shows THEIR name climbing. That shot gets attention.
- Scroll in slow, steady swipes, one screen per line of the script. Let every number finish counting (about 1.5 s) and every animation land before you move on.
- The bar under the header ("Step 3 of 8 · Look legit") shows viewers where they are. Keep it in the crop.
- The orange 💬 boxes are your audience questions. Pause on them and **read them to the camera**. They drive comments, and comments make the ad cheaper.
- Burn in captions. Keep them short and in the middle third, above the phone.
