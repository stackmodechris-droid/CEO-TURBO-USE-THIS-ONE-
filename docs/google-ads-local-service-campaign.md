# Google Ads: "Google Ads for Local Service" campaign

Goal: booked calls from local service business owners (appliance repair, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, handymen) who search for help with Google Ads or leads.

Character limits are checked: headlines 30, descriptions 90, sitelink text 25, sitelink lines 35, callouts 25.

## Campaign settings
- **Name:** CEO Turbo | Google Ads for Local Service
- **Type:** Search only. Turn off Display Network and Search Partners.
- **Locations:** United States, "Presence: people in or regularly in your locations".
- **Schedule:** Mon–Fri 7am–8pm, Sat 9am–3pm (Eastern).
- **Budget:** $12/day. Keep "CEO Turbo | Brand" at $1–3/day.
- **Bidding:** Maximize clicks with a max CPC cap of $3 (or Manual CPC at $3). Switch to Maximize conversions after 15+ real conversions.
- **Conversion goals:** Primary = Book appointment + Submit lead form. Contact (phone click) = Secondary.
- **End date:** none (the old campaigns end Oct 26, 2026, so extend or replace them).
- **Pause:** CEO Turbo | NFC Review Cards, CEO Turbo | Calls | US Niche.

## When to change things
- Don't touch bids or budget until each ad group has ~30 clicks.
- If one ad group gets the bookings, move the budget to it and pause the others.
- 0 bookings after ~80 clicks: the problem is the page or the offer, not the keywords. Watch Clarity recordings of the landing page before changing the ads.
