# Cal.com Booking Questions - Office Transformation Service

**Goal:** Create a comprehensive but simple question set for office transformation consultations that gathers all necessary information for accurate quoting while being easy for older adults and lower technical literacy users.

---

## EVENT TYPE DETAILS

**Event Name:** Office Transformation Consultation
**Slug:** office-transformation-consultation
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Location:** In Person
**Description:** Free 30-minute consultation to discuss your office transformation needs. We'll cover your space, timeline, equipment, and budget.

---

## RECOMMENDED QUESTIONS (In Order)

### Question 1: What type of space needs work?
**Type:** Multiple Choice
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** What type of space is this?

**Options:**
- Office space
- Warehouse
- Medical office or clinic
- School or classroom
- Retail store
- Other (I'll explain)

**Why ask:** Determines the complexity and special requirements (medical needs HIPAA compliance, schools need durability, etc.)

---

### Question 2: How big is the space?
**Type:** Multiple Choice
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** About how big is the space?

**Options:**
- Small (under 1,000 sq ft)
- Medium (1,000 to 3,000 sq ft)
- Large (3,000 to 5,000 sq ft)
- Very large (over 5,000 sq ft)
- Not sure - I'll measure

**Why ask:** Determines pricing tier and timeline. Small = Basic package, Medium = Standard, Large = Complete.

---

### Question 3: What work do you need done?
**Type:** Multiple Choice (allow multiple selections)
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** What do you need us to do?

**Options:**
- Build walls or framing
- Install security cameras
- Set up internet and network
- Build or set up computers
- Clean up after work
- All of the above

**Why ask:** Determines which services are needed and affects pricing.

---

### Question 4: How many computers or workstations?
**Type:** Multiple Choice
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** How many computers or workstations need setup?

**Options:**
- 1 to 5 computers
- 6 to 10 computers
- 11 to 20 computers
- More than 20 computers
- No computers needed

**Why ask:** Affects computer building labor and network requirements.

---

### Question 5: Do you need security cameras?
**Type:** Multiple Choice
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** Do you need security cameras?

**Options:**
- Yes, need full camera system
- Yes, add cameras to existing system
- No, cameras already installed
- Not sure - need advice

**Why ask:** Cameras are a major part of the service and affect pricing significantly.

---

### Question 6: When do you need this done?
**Type:** Multiple Choice
**Required:** Yes
**Label:** When do you need the work done?

**Options:**
- As soon as possible
- Within 2 weeks
- Within 1 month

**Why ask:** Determines scheduling urgency and affects availability.

## QUESTIONS TO AVOID (Do NOT Ask)

### ❌ Technical Questions
- "What type of network cabling do you need?" (Too technical)
- "Do you need Cat6 or Cat7 cabling?" (Too technical)
- "What router model do you have?" (Too technical)
- "Do you need PoE cameras?" (Too technical)
- "What's your IP addressing scheme?" (Way too technical)

**Why avoid:** Target audience is older adults and lower technical literacy. They won't know these answers. Ask in simple terms instead.

---

### ❌ Irrelevant Questions
- "How did you hear about us?" (Nice to know but not needed for quoting)
- "What's your company website?" (Not needed for the quote)
- "How many employees do you have?" (Not directly relevant to the work scope)
- "What's your industry?" (Nice to know but doesn't affect the quote)
- "Do you have a referral code?" (Not relevant)

**Why avoid:** These don't affect the pricing or scope of work. Keep it focused on what's needed for the quote.

---

### ❌ Overly Detailed Questions
- "Exactly how many linear feet framing?" (Too detailed for initial consult)
- "What brand of cameras do you prefer?" (Can discuss in consult)
- "Do you need NVR or cloud storage?" (Can discuss in consult)
- "What's your internet speed?" (Can discuss in consult)
- "Do you need VLANs?" (Too technical)

**Why avoid:** These details can be covered during the 30-minute consultation. The booking questions should be high-level screening.

---

### ❌ Questions Assumed in Other Answers
- "Do you need Wi-Fi?" (Covered in "What work do you need done?")
- "Do you need cable management?" (Always included, don't ask)
- "Do you need cleanup?" (Always included, don't ask)
- "Do you need software installed?" (Always included, don't ask)

**Why avoid:** These are standard inclusions. Don't ask about things that are always part of the service.

---

### ❌ Yes/No Questions That Don't Help
- "Are you the decision maker?" (Can discuss in consult)
- "Have you worked with contractors before?" (Not relevant)
- "Is this a new building or existing?" (Can discuss in consult)
- "Do you have a floor plan?" (Nice to have but not required)

**Why avoid:** These don't help determine pricing or scope efficiently.

## QUESTION ORDER RATIONALE

**Screening Questions (1-3):**
- Type of space, size, work needed
- Quickly determines if this is a good fit and roughly what package

**Scope Questions (4-5):**
- Computers, cameras
- Refines the scope within the package

**Timing Questions (6):**
- When needed
- Determines urgency and scheduling

**Budget Questions (7):**
- Budget range
- Pre-qualifies and sets expectations

**Location Questions (8):**
- Service area confirmation
- Determines travel fees

**Materials Questions (9):**
- Who buys materials
- Critical pricing clarification

**Contact Questions (10-12):**
- Phone, email, name
- Required for communication

**Optional Question (13):**
- Additional notes
- Catch-all for special cases

---

## BEST PRACTICES

### Keep It Simple
- Use everyday language
- Avoid technical terms
- Provide clear options
- Allow "Not sure" as an option

### Keep It Short
- Maximum 13 questions
- Most are multiple choice (easy to answer)
- Only 1 required text question (name)
- 1 optional text question (additional notes)

### Keep It Relevant
- Every question affects the quote or scheduling
- No "nice to know" questions
- No questions that can be answered in the consult

### Keep It Friendly
- Use simple, direct language
- Don't sound bureaucratic
- Make it feel like a conversation

## ALTERNATIVE: SHORTER QUESTION SET (For Faster Booking)

If you want fewer questions (higher conversion, less information):

**Essential 7 Questions:**
1. What type of space? (Office, warehouse, medical, school, retail)
2. How big? (Small, medium, large, very large)
3. What work needed? (Walls, cameras, internet, computers, cleanup, all)
4. When needed? (ASAP, 2 weeks, 1 month, planning)
5. Budget? (Under $2k, $2-4k, $4-6k, over $6k)
6. Your phone number
7. Your email

**Trade-off:** Faster booking but less information before the consult. Details covered in the 30-minute call.

## TESTING THE QUESTIONS

**Test with target audience:**
1. Can an older adult answer all questions in under 3 minutes?
2. Do they understand what each question is asking?
3. Are any questions confusing or ambiguous?
4. Do they feel comfortable answering?
5. Would they prefer fewer questions?

**Success criteria:**
- 80%+ complete the form without abandoning
- Average completion time under 3 minutes
- No questions marked as "confusing" in feedback
- High booking completion rate

## CAL.COM CONFIGURATION

**Advanced → Questions Settings:**
- Enable "Required" for questions 1-12
- Question 13 is optional
- Allow multiple selections for Question 3
- Use "Multiple Choice" for most questions (easier than dropdown)
- Set question order as listed above

**Redirect After Booking:**
- URL: `https://ceoturbo.com/booked?service=office-transformation`
- This fires the `book_call` tracking event

**Confirmation Message:**
```
Your consultation is booked! We'll call you at the scheduled time at 470-502-9543. If you need to reschedule, use the link in your confirmation email.
```
