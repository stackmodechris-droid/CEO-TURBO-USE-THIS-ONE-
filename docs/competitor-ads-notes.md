# Competitor ads — what review-card sellers run right now

Source: Meta Ad Library (public), US, active ads for "nfc google review card", read on Oct 12 2026. Patterns only. We don't copy their ads.

## What they run
| Advertiser | Hook / offer | What stands out |
|---|---|---|
| Merchynt | "Free NFC Google review card. Clip this one." First card free, free US shipping, no setup or monthly fee. | **Arrives already programmed** to your Google profile. Plain, unbranded card. Free first card removes all risk. |
| ZappyCards | "Your competitor down the street has 2,000 Google reviews. You have 34." Also "Google ranks businesses on what the review says." | Competitor-envy hook, **90-day money-back guarantee**, up to 50% off the first card, "no risk if you don't get a 20% boost", clip-on card for techs, "Over 18,000 reviews collected". |
| Tap Grow | "$50 Local Setup — Card Included", local, they program and deliver it. | Done-for-you convenience for local businesses. |

## Where CEO Turbo is weaker (be honest about it)
1. **Programming:** ours ships blank and the buyer programs it in about 2 minutes. Merchynt ships it already set up. This is the biggest friction.
2. **Guarantee:** our return window is 5 days. ZappyCards advertises 90 days. A longer, clearer guarantee converts better.
3. **First-order risk:** Merchynt's first card is free.

## Where we can win
1. **Setup help is real:** a free setup guide, a review-link finder, a printable QR, and a human at (470) 502-9543.
2. **The tutorial angle:** none of them teach. Your 9:16 tutorial (`/get-more-reviews`) explains why reviews matter before selling.
3. **Agency upsell:** a card buyer can become a retainer client. They can't offer that.
4. **Hooks to reuse as patterns:** the "your competitor has more reviews" envy hook (already in the /get-more-reviews poll), and "no monthly fee, no app".

## Offers worth testing (your decision; I haven't changed the site)
- **"We program it for you":** ask for the Google review link at checkout (the buy box already collects the business name) and write it before shipping. This is the single best conversion lever against Merchynt. It costs you a few minutes per order.
- **Longer return window.** The 5-day policy is now set in Merchant Center. If you extend it, change it there and on the site together.
- **Proof:** once real buyers exist, add their reviews. Don't show counts like "18,000 reviews" until you have real numbers.
