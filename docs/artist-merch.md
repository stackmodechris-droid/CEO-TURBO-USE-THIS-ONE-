# Artist merch — what it is and how to sell it

Page: **https://www.ceoturbo.com/artist-merch** · Shopify product `15419989033195` (handle `custom-artist-merch-t-shirts`) · fulfillment, profit and tracking: **`docs/company-shirts.md`** (same workflow, Design IDs start with `AM-`).

## The offer

Artists design their own merch in 2 minutes and order stock for shows, drops and giveaways. No minimum. From $24.99, free US shipping, 10% / 15% / 20% off at 12 / 24 / 50 shirts. They sell at $30 at shows and keep $10–15 a shirt — the profit table on the page shows the math.

What the designer does (the Printify-style "product creator", built into the site):

- Upload cover art / logo / photo → auto white-background removal, low-res warning.
- **Drag** the art on the shirt, size slider, up/down + left/right fine-tune, **rotate** ±45°, **Center** reset.
- Artist name in **6 fonts** (Clean, Poster, Tall, Marker, Gothic, Serif) + a small line (album, @handle).
- **Back print** (+$7): title ("WORLD TOUR 2026"), **tour dates / track list one per line** (up to 14), or **different back art**.
- 6 colors (Black, White, Navy, Sport Grey, Maroon, Forest Green), short or long sleeve, S–3XL.
- Live mockup front/back; the print file is the mockup at 300 DPI, so what they see is what prints.

## Who to sell it to

1. **Every artist you already work with** (`/artist` audience, Brand Boost calls, BIGZO etc.): "Want 24 shirts with the new cover for the release show? I'll set it up." Easiest first sales.
2. Independent rappers / bands / DJs / producers on IG + TikTok — the paid ads in `docs/ads/meta-tiktok-shirts.md`.
3. Local venues and promoters in Atlanta: they know who's playing next month. Offer a promoter $3 a shirt for every artist they send.

## Upsells that already exist on the site

- Artist growth (ads, website, socials) on `/artist` — the merch page links to it and `/artist` links back ("Sell merch → Design your merch").
- Company shirts for their label / studio / crew.

## Rules

Only print art the artist owns. No other artists' names, brand logos, sports teams, characters. If an upload looks like it isn't theirs, text them before ordering from the printer (the page and FAQ say so).
