# /plan tutorial ad — 9:16 script

**Format:** your face in the top half, ceoturbo.com/plan on a phone in the bottom half. You scroll while you talk.
**Goal:** stop the scroll, teach something useful, prove it with real numbers, then send them to the free plan.
**Emotion:** fear of losing customers to someone worse → relief (there's a simple fix) → trust (real money, real dashboards) → access (you talk to the CEO).

Every number on screen is real, and so is every number in the script. Don't round them up when you talk.

---

## Recording setup

- Record the screen on your phone (or Chrome DevTools at **390 × 844**) at **ceoturbo.com/plan**.
- Wait for each big number to finish counting before you scroll on. Each one counts up in about 1.5 seconds.
- Scroll in slow, steady swipes. One section per line of the script.
- The thin bar under the header ("Step 2 of 5 · Look worth it") tells viewers where you are. Make sure it's visible in the crop.
- Burn captions into the video. Most people watch with the sound off. Keep them short, in the middle third, above the bottom half.
- Look at the camera, not the screen, on every line marked **(camera)**.

## Three opening hooks to A/B test (first 3 seconds)

1. **Fear:** "If a customer Googles what you do right now, they're finding your competitor. Here's why."
2. **Curiosity:** "There are 5 things standing between your business and a phone that won't stop ringing."
3. **Proof:** "This is my real Stripe account. 152 payments this year. Here's exactly what I do for businesses."

Run each one as its own ad with the same body. Keep the winner after about $50–100 spend each.

## Rules (keep the ad approved and trusted)

- Never promise a client result ("you'll make $10K"). The Stripe numbers are **CEO Turbo's** revenue, and the page says so.
- Don't say "Google partner" or imply Google made the card. The card links to your Google review page.
- Don't ask customers to pay for reviews or offer anything in exchange for them. Ask every customer the same way.
