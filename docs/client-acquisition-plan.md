## Microsoft (cards) — rules
- The campaign **Review Cards - Search - US** is built and paused. Turn it on (ads.microsoft.com → enable ad groups, ads, keywords, then the campaign).
- **Day 3:** open Search terms. Add a negative for anything that isn't a business buying a card.
- **Day 7:** turn the CPC cap down to $0.80 if clicks are expensive; add winning terms as Exact.
- **Be honest about margin:** a card nets about $18–$26 after cost. A $1 click needs a 5% buy rate to break even. Expect cards to run near break-even at first. The real prize is the card buyer who becomes a retainer lead.
- **Booked-call search campaign (later):** agency keywords in Los Angeles cost several dollars a click, so wait until cards are selling. Use phrase match: "website design los angeles", "google business profile optimization", "seo for small business los angeles", "website for new business", "artist website", "producer website". Skip broad terms like "get more customers" and "start your own business": too general, so people click and never buy. Rank for those with guide articles instead (your guides already target them).

## Free outreach (the fastest way to clients) — 20 a day
1. Search Google Maps for businesses in LA with **under 30 reviews** or a thin profile (barbers, salons, restaurants, contractors, auto shops, gyms).
2. Send a short message (Instagram DM or email):
   > Hey [name], I checked [business] on Google. You're showing up, but with [X] reviews you're losing calls to the shop with more. I made a free 60-second check that shows exactly where. Want me to send it? — Christopher, CEO Turbo, Atlanta
3. When they reply, send **ceoturbo.com/free-audit**. Then offer the free Brand Boost call.
4. Track them in a simple sheet: sent / replied / call booked / closed.

## After a lead comes in
- **Answer in 5 minutes.** Call or text. A fast reply books more calls than any ad tweak.
- **Call outline (30 minutes):** show their site, Google profile and reviews live. Name the ONE money move. Quote one package + one plan from the pricing page. Ask for the setup fee on the call.
- **Close:** 50% to start, 50% at launch (your published terms).
