# Site Simplification Prompt - Older/Low-IQ Audience Optimization

**Goal:** Simplify the website for older adults and users with lower technical literacy while maintaining professionalism and conversion optimization.

---

## TARGET AUDIENCE ANALYSIS

**Characteristics:**
- Age 45-75+
- Lower technical literacy
- Prefer phone over online forms
- Need clear, simple language
- Want immediate answers
- Trust familiar patterns
- May have vision/hearing challenges
- Value simplicity over features

**Behavior:**
- Will call if phone number is obvious
- Abandon complex forms
- Don't understand technical jargon
- Need step-by-step guidance
- Want to speak to a human
- Trust local businesses

---

## SIMPLIFICATION PRINCIPLES

### 1. Language Simplification

**BEFORE (Too Complex):**
- "Enterprise-grade network infrastructure"
- "Multi-zone Wi-Fi with guest and staff networks"
- "Server setup and network storage"
- "Cable management with labeled racks"

**AFTER (Simple):**
- "We set up your internet"
- "Wi-Fi for everyone"
- "We organize all the wires"
- "Everything labeled and easy to find"

**Rules:**
- Use 1-2 syllable words when possible
- Avoid technical acronyms (explain or remove)
- Use active voice
- Short sentences (under 15 words)
- One idea per sentence

### 2. Visual Simplification

**Reduce Clutter:**
- Remove decorative elements that don't add value
- Increase white space
- Larger fonts (minimum 16px body, 24px headings)
- Higher contrast (dark text on light background)
- Clear section breaks

**Simplify Navigation:**
- Fewer menu items
- Clear labels (not clever/cute)
- Breadcrumbs on every page
- "Back to Home" button

### 3. Call-to-Action Simplification

**Current:**
- "Book a free call about office transformation"
- "Which do I need? Get my free plan"

**Simplified:**
- "Call Now: 470-502-9543"
- "Get a Free Quote"
- "Book a Visit"

**Rules:**
- Action verbs first
- Phone number visible
- One clear action per section
- Large, tappable buttons (mobile)

### 4. Pricing Simplification

**Current:**
- "Installation labor for medium spaces. Materials and equipment are supplied by you or funded up front."

**Simplified:**
- "Labor Only: $2,999"
- "You buy materials, we do the work"
- "Or pay us upfront, we buy everything"

**Add Visual Aids:**
- Large price numbers
- Checkmarks for what's included
- Simple comparison table
- "What's included" list with icons

## CONTENT REWRITING EXAMPLES

### Service Description

**BEFORE:**
"CEO Turbo transforms empty spaces into fully operational offices. We handle the labor: office framing and build-out, security camera installation, network setup, computer building and configuration, and cleanup. Pricing is a flat one-time labor quote with no monthly contract. You supply the materials and equipment, or fund them up front and we order them for you. Serving the Atlanta metro area and up to two hours from Duluth, GA."

**AFTER:**
"We turn empty offices into ready-to-work spaces. We do the work: build walls, install cameras, set up internet, build computers, and clean up. You pay once for our labor. No monthly fees. You buy the materials, or give us money and we buy them for you. We serve Atlanta and nearby cities."

"Installation labor for medium spaces. Materials and equipment are supplied by you or funded up front."

"We serve Atlanta and cities within 2 hours of Duluth. Call us and we'll tell you if we can help."

## ACCESSIBILITY IMPROVEMENTS

### Visual
- Minimum font size: 18px body, 24px headings
- Line height: 1.6 for readability
- Contrast ratio: 4.5:1 minimum
- No light gray text on white

### Cognitive
- One main idea per section
- Clear headings (H1, H2, H3 hierarchy)
- Consistent layout across pages
- Predictable navigation

### Motor
- Large click targets (44x44px minimum)
- No hover-only interactions
- Keyboard accessible
- Clear focus states

## TESTING WITH TARGET AUDIENCE

**Test Questions:**
1. Can you find the phone number within 5 seconds?
2. Do you understand what this service does?
3. Do you know how much it costs?
4. Do you know if they serve your area?
5. Would you feel comfortable calling them?

**Test Method:**
- Ask 3-5 people in target age group
- Give them 30 seconds to scan the page
- Ask the 5 questions above
- Note where they get confused

**Success Criteria:**
- 80%+ can find phone in 5 seconds
- 80%+ understand the service
- 80%+ know pricing range
- 80%+ know service area
- 60%+ would feel comfortable calling

## MEASURING SUCCESS

**Metrics to Track:**
- Time on page (should increase = understanding)
- Bounce rate (should decrease)
- Phone call rate (should increase)
- Form abandonment (should decrease)
- Mobile conversion rate (should increase)

**A/B Test:**
- Version A: Current site
- Version B: Simplified site
- Measure: Phone calls, bookings, time on page

**Expected Results:**
- 20-30% increase in phone calls
- 15-25% decrease in bounce rate
- 10-20% increase in mobile conversions
- Better feedback from older users

---

## NOTES

- Don't "dumb down" - simplify for clarity, not condescension
- Maintain professionalism while being accessible
- Test with actual target audience, not assumptions
- Accessibility helps everyone, not just older users
- Simple language often converts better for all audiences
- Phone number should be the most visible element
- Trust signals (reviews, warranty) remain important
