1. Meta (Events Manager)

1.1 Pixel settings (Events Manager → your pixel → Settings)

  • Track events automatically without code: Off.
  • Automatic events (AI): Off. Your own code sends exact events; these would add guesses and duplicates.
  • Automatic advanced matching: On.

1.2 Shopify connection

  • Shopify admin → Apps → Facebook & Instagram → connect pixel 3232222473609236. Data sharing: Maximum.
  • This app is the only thing that sends Meta "Purchase". Do not add Meta to the custom pixel or anywhere else in Shopify.

1.3 Check events arrive (Events Manager → Test events)

  • Enter ceoturbo.com and open it: PageView, ViewContent appear.
  • Run a free check with fake details: Lead appears twice (Browser + Server), marked deduplicated.
  • Book a test call: Schedule appears twice (Browser + Server), deduplicated.
  • Check "Event match quality" later in Overview. Aim for Good or better on Lead, Schedule and Purchase.

1.4 Campaign setup

  • Agency ads: conversion event = Schedule (use Lead while you have few bookings per week).
  • Card ads: conversion event = Purchase.
  • Send ads to your own booking page (ceoturbo.com/brand-boost or /book), not the raw cal.com link.
  • URL parameters on every ad: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}

6.2 Paid orders into your Google Sheet (webhook)

9. Keep it accurate

  • Weekly: compare Google Ads / Microsoft / Meta conversion counts with your Booked Calls tab. Small differences are normal; big gaps mean a tag broke.
  • Booked calls from the raw cal.com link are counted by Meta and your sheet (webhook) but not by Google or Microsoft. Always send ad traffic to your own booking page.
  • If you run ads to the EU, UK or California, add a cookie consent banner. It isn't set up yet.
  • Your Google Sheet and Apps Script stay private: keep sharing "Restricted" and use 2-step verification on the owner account.
